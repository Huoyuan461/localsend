Zhichuan: send files between your phone and computer. Original quality, no compression, free.

[Why you need Zhichuan]
- Tired of messaging apps compressing your videos into mush? Zhichuan transfers over your local network at full speed, in original quality. A 1GB video takes seconds.
- Need to send files from Android to iPhone? AirDrop doesn't do Android. Zhichuan connects Android ↔ iPhone ↔ Windows ↔ Mac — every platform talks to every other.
- No internet? As long as your devices are on the same Wi-Fi (it even works without internet access), you can still send.

[What it is]
Zhichuan is a free local file transfer tool: install the app on your phones, tablets and computers, join the same network, and send files, photos and videos to each other. No account, no cloud upload, no ads.

- Original quality: what you send is what arrives, never compressed
- Fast: direct local transfer, as fast as your Wi-Fi allows
- No size limits: multi-GB videos, whole folders, in one go
- Private: end-to-end encrypted, files travel only between your devices, never through a server
- Free: free for personal use, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions

[How to use]
Open Zhichuan on both devices → pick files → tap send → the other side taps accept. That's it.

Zhichuan is based on the open-source project LocalSend (Apache-2.0).
